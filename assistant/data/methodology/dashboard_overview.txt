ESG SUPPLY CHAIN RISK DASHBOARD - OVERVIEW

Purpose
The ESG Supply Chain Risk Dashboard is a tool for companies to understand and quantify the Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) risks embedded in their supply chains. Users upload a CSV file listing their materials, the countries they are sourced from, and the associated costs. The dashboard then produces cost-weighted risk scores across five ESG indicators and visualizes them as radar charts, bar charts, and a geographic heatmap.

The five ESG indicators
1. CO2 Emissions (Environmental): CO2 per capita from Our World in Data / Global Carbon Project. Measures carbon intensity of a country's economy.
2. Corruption (Governance): Inverted CPI score from Transparency International CPI 2025. Measures governance quality and rule of law.
3. Forced Labor (Social): Modern slavery prevalence per 1,000 population from Walk Free Global Slavery Index 2023. Measures the risk of forced or coerced labor.
4. Water Stress (Environmental): Projected water withdrawal-to-availability ratio from WRI Aqueduct 4.0 (2030 BAU). Measures physical water scarcity risk.
5. Child Labor (Social): Percentage of children aged 5-17 in child labor from UNICEF Jun 2025. Measures child labor prevalence.

All scores normalized to 0-10 scale
Each indicator is normalized using min-max normalization across all 214 countries in the dataset, so that 0 = lowest risk in dataset and 10 = highest risk in dataset. This makes all five indicators directly comparable on the same radar chart.

Cost-weighted risk scores
The dashboard calculates cost-weighted risk scores. Each material's risk contribution is proportional to its share of total supply chain cost. A material that accounts for 30% of total spend contributes 30% of the weight to each indicator score. This reflects the reality that high-spend suppliers have more influence over a company's overall ESG risk profile than low-spend ones.

Overall risk score
The overall risk score for a country is the simple average of all available indicator scores. If a country is missing data for one or more indicators, the average is taken over the available indicators. Countries missing all indicator data are excluded from the analysis.

Input data format
Users upload a CSV with three required columns:
  - material: the name of the material, component, or service
  - country: the country where the material is sourced (must match country names in the ESG database)
  - cost_usd: the annual cost in US dollars for that material/country combination

Outputs
  - Radar chart: overall supply chain risk profile across 5 indicators
  - Bar chart: per-material risk breakdown for a selected indicator
  - World map: choropleth showing overall risk by sourcing country
  - Data table: full detail with downloadable CSV
